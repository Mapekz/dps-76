# Fire rate / reload / capacity sensitivity review (2026-07-15)

Why fire-rate and reload-speed effects look so strong in the calc, whether the
math behind them is right, and where Quad actually lands. Every number in this
document was generated by the real engine (`resolveLoadout` →
`computeScenarios`, `evaluateSuggestions`) on greedy-optimized builds — the
generation script lives in the session scratchpad; the method is reproducible
from the tables below.

**TL;DR verdicts**

1. **The weakpoint fold is correct** as implemented and stays unchanged:
   `bodyPartMult × (1 + Σ weakpointBonus)` — Pin-Pointer's/Gunslinger Expert
   add with *each other*, and the summed factor multiplies the body-part mult
   (associatively, the whole thing is one more damage multiplier).
2. **The suggestion ranking is not skewed toward fire rate/reload.** On all
   four audited regimes, 7–8 of the top-10 movers are plain damage (perHit)
   changes. Timing mods only top the list where the math genuinely favors
   them (per-shell lever action at 72% reload fraction), and the #1
   "reload-looking" suggestion elsewhere (Swift Magazine) turns out to be a
   **capacity** mod in disguise (see §7).
3. **Quad ranks exactly where the closed form says it should**: +13% on a
   Fixer, +7% on a Gatling Plasma, **+0.0% on per-shell reloaders** (capacity
   cancels out of sustained DPS entirely), and it *overtakes* Two Shot late
   in a build as the additive dbm pool dilutes.
4. **One real model risk stands**: reload bonuses are folded as a rate
   divisor (`t/1.x`). If the game time-scales instead (`t×(1−x)`), the calc
   *understates* reload mods — the two semantics diverge hard at stacked
   bonuses (§6). Stopwatch protocol tracked in `#2`.
5. **Fast Fighter was unmodeled and now works** (§9): it grants half your
   bonus movement speed as reload speed, auto-derived from modeled move-speed
   sources (Speed Demon +20/+25%, Wasteland Fish Sandwich +20%).

## 1. The formulas under review

```
burstDps     = perHit × fireRate                        (mag dump, no reload)
sustainedDps = perHit × N / (N/F + R)
             N = shots per mag,  F = fire rate,  R = reload seconds
```

Define the two numbers that govern everything below:

- **T = N/F** — mag-dump seconds
- **r = R/(T+R)** — the **reload fraction**: how much of a full cycle is
  spent reloading

Then, to first order:

| Knob | Burst DPS | Sustained DPS |
|---|---|---|
| +x% fire rate | **+x%** (pure multiplier) | +x·(1−r)% |
| Reload time −y% | 0 | +y·r% (approx, small y) |
| +p points of dbm into a pool of size D | +p/D % | +p/D % |
| Whole-damage mult +x% | **+x%** | **+x%** |
| Capacity ×k (mag reloader) | 0 | ×1/(1 − (1−1/k)·r) |
| Capacity ×k (per-shell reloader) | 0 | **×1.000** — R ∝ N, k cancels |

Fire rate is the only knob that multiplies **burst** DPS outright. For
**sustained** DPS every timing knob is throttled by the reload fraction —
and additive damage perks are throttled by how crowded the dbm parenthesis
already is (**additive dilution**: the +25% card added to a build whose
parenthesis already sums to 5.3× is worth +4.7%, not +25%).

## 2. The four audited regimes

Builds were greedy-assembled by the suggestion engine itself (apply the best
legal suggestion until gains fall under 0.5%; SPECIAL pinned at 15s so the
perk budget never gates the comparison; VATS scenario, default 25% crit).

| | Fixer (auto rifle) | Gatling Plasma (big-mag heavy) | Gauss Shotgun (charging) | Lever Action (per-shell) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| perHit | 381 | 366 | 3376 | 1367 |
| fire rate /s | 9.77 | 11.36 | 0.87 | 2.50 |
| shots/mag | 90 | 750 | 16 | 6 |
| mag dump T | 9.2s | 66.0s | 18.4s | 2.4s |
| reload R | 1.7s | 6.2s | 2.1s | 6.1s (per-shell ×6) |

| burst DPS | 3726 | 4162 | 2936 | 3418 |
| sustained DPS | 3149 | 3804 | 2631 | 970 |

The regimes span r from 8.6% to 71.6% — that one number predicts every
timing-mod ranking below.

## 3. Head-to-head elasticity (engine-measured, single knob on the finished build)

Sustained-DPS gain from one knob turn:

| Knob | Fixer | Gatling Plasma | Gauss Shotgun | Lever Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| +10% fire rate | +8.3% | +9.1% | **+1.1%** | +2.6% |
| +10% reload bonus | +0.8% | +0.7% | +0.8% | **+4.0%** |
| +25% dbm damage card | +4.7% | +4.6% | +3.9% | +4.0% |
| +25% whole-damage mult | +25.0% | +25.0% | +25.0% | +25.0% |
| Quad (capacity ×4) | +13.2% | +6.9% | +8.5% | **+0.0%** |
| +50% crit damage bonus | +2.4% | +2.3% | +1.9% | +1.3% |
| +25% weakpoint bonus (aiming) | +25.0% | +21.8% | +20.1% | +22.0% |

Burst DPS: +10% fire rate = +10.0% everywhere except the Gauss Shotgun
(+1.2% — charging weapons' charge hold is wall-clock and speed-immune; only
the post-shot animation tail divides by speed). Reload/capacity knobs move
burst by exactly 0.

**Checks against the closed forms** (§1): Fixer fire rate 10%×(1−.155) =
+8.5% ≈ engine +8.3% ✓. Quad on Fixer 1/(1−0.75×.155) = +13.2% ✓ exact.
Lever Quad: R ∝ N ⇒ +0.0% ✓. Reload +0.1 on Fixer whose fold already sits at
1.89: r·b/(m+b) = .155×.1/1.99 = +0.8% ✓.

**Readings:**

- Fire rate IS a genuine near-pure multiplier — on burst always, on
  sustained wherever r is small. It *rivals whole-damage multipliers* and
  beats every additive damage card on crowded builds. That is real game
  math, not a calc bias.
- Reload bonuses on magazine weapons are the WEAKEST knob in the table
  (+0.7–0.8%) — the calc does not overrate them; late-build Swift/Rapid
  swaps only win because everything else has diluted even further.
- Weakpoint bonuses (your Gunslinger/Pin-Pointer's example) hold their full
  multiplicative value (+20–25% — diluted only by *other* weakpoint bonuses
  already in that additive sub-pool, e.g. the Gatling Plasma build's
  existing +14.7% weakpoint sources cut the +25 points to +21.8%).

## 4. Quad, precisely

For magazine reloaders, capacity ×k multiplies sustained DPS by
`1/(1 − (1−1/k)·r)` — it converts reload downtime into uptime and does
nothing else. Consequences the engine reproduces exactly:

- Worth the most where reloads dominate: hypothetical r = 40% weapon → Quad
  = +43%. Fixer (r=15.5%) → +13.2%. Gatling Plasma (r=8.6%) → +6.9%.
- **Worthless on per-shell reloaders** (lever/pump/single actions): reload
  time scales with shots loaded, so N cancels out of `N/(N/F + N·R₁)` —
  the engine shows +0.0% and that is correct, not a bug.
- Quad **cannot beat a whole-damage multiplier** unless r > (roughly) the
  multiplier's size ÷ (0.75 × (1+size)); it beats *additive* cards easily
  late-build: the Fixer greedy path swapped ★1 Two Shot → Quad at step 20
  (+2.4% at that point) because Two Shot's +0.75 dbm had diluted into a
  ~5× parenthesis while Quad's amortization multiplier never dilutes.
- Burst DPS: Quad contributes 0. Any ranking that used burst as the metric
  would never suggest it.

## 5. Where the "skew" impression comes from

Three effects compound into "fire rate and reload always float to the top":

1. **Multipliers don't dilute; adders do.** Every +X% damage card lands in
   one shared parenthesis. By mid-build that parenthesis is 4–6×, so a "25%"
   card is really ~5%. Fire rate (and whole-damage, and weakpoint-on-a-clean
   -pool) keep their sticker value. The calc is faithfully modeling additive
   dilution, which the in-game card text hides.
2. **High-r weapons genuinely live and die by reload.** On the lever action
   (r=71.6%), 3★ Swift at +8.7% sustained ranked #1 — correctly: shaving a
   6-second-per-6-shots reload beats any diluted damage card.
3. **Mislabeled capacity mods.** The #1 "reload" suggestion on the Fixer —
   Swift Magazine, +8.6% — is, per its ESM properties, `capacity +50%,
   reload −20% (inherited) +20% (own) = net 0`. Its entire gain is Quad-style
   amortization. The panel's decomposition (mag +33%, perHit −1.5%,
   reload 0) shows this; the display name does not.

## 6. The one live math risk: divide vs time-scale reload

`reloadSec = animationReloadSec / reloadSpeed` is an ASSUMPTION
(`sustain.ts`, flagged `reloadApproximate`). The alternative reading of
"+x% reload speed" is `animationReloadSec × (1 − x)`. They diverge fast:

| Stacked bonus | divide (current) | time-scale |
|---|---|---|
| +20% | −16.7% time | −20.0% time |
| +40% | −28.6% | −40.0% |
| +70% | −41.2% | −70.0% |
| +90% | −47.4% | −90.0% |

On the audited builds the end-state difference is small where r is small
(Gatling Plasma: 3804 vs 3807 sustained) but dramatic where r is big — the
lever-action build sustains **970 under divide vs 1624 under time-scale**
(+67%!). If the game time-scales, the calc currently *understates* every
reload source, most on exactly the weapons where reload matters most.

Divide is the better prior: it is the standard AV-multiplier reading of
`WeapReloadSpeedMult` (the AV defaults to 1.0 and OMOD `ReloadSpeed`
properties MUL/ADD onto the same 1.0-baseline weapon stat), and time-scaling
explodes at stacked bonuses (Speed Demon 0.4 + Gun Tricks 0.3 + Swift Mag
0.2 → −90% reload time is not believable in-game). But it is measurable:
**.44 revolver, +30% bonus → 2.56s/shell (divide) vs 2.33s (time-scale);
stacked +60% as a ghoul → −37.5% vs −60%** — protocol tracked in `#2`.

## 8. Weakpoint math — confirmed correct (resolved this session)

```
damage = scaledBase × (dbm parenthesis) × Π wholeDamage
         × bodyPartMult × (1 + Σ weakpointBonus)   [only when bodyPartMult > 1]
```

Head 2.0×, Pin-Pointer's +50%, Gunslinger Expert +25% → 2.0 × 1.75 = 3.5×.
The bonuses are additive with each other (they share the 1.0-based pool) and
multiplicative with the body-part mult and with total damage — user-confirmed
as the intended semantics; bucket keeps the name `weakpointBonus`.

## 10. What changed in the calc this session

- **`moveSpeedBonus` bucket + curve input** (bootstrap-folded like
  onslaughtMaxStacks, threaded on `ResolveContext`): AV `SpeedMult` now
  routes to it at scale 0.01 (`normalize/mgef.ts`).
- **Fast Fighter override** (`perk-overrides.ts`): reloadSpeed ADD, identity
  curve on `moveSpeedBonus` × 0.5 — description-sourced (no ESM effects
  exist). End-to-end: Fixer 1.1765 → +Speed Demon 1.4765 → +Fast Fighter
  1.5765. Negative net move speed clamps to 0 (never slows reload).
- Sprint-gated SpeedMult perks (Freight Train, Dead Man Sprinting, Gun
  Runner…) extract with unresolved IsSprinting-style gates and stay inert
  by design; unconditional sources (Wasteland Fish Sandwich +20%) feed Fast
  Fighter automatically. Follow-up sweep: `docs/move-speed-census.md`.
- New measurement issues: divide-vs-time-scale reload stopwatch (`#2`), Fast
  Fighter conversion check (`#69`).
